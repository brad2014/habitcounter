# HabitCounter

You know those handheld tally counters, where you click and a counter
goes up?

This is that.

For example, your doctor wants you to note how many glasses of water
you have per day. Create a counter called "water", and every time
you have a drink, tap the +1. The app will timestamp each tap, and
tell you how many glasses of water you had in the past hour, the
past day, the past month. Tap a number to see a graph over time.

You don't even need to open the app. Just edit your counter to "pin" it.
That creates a shortcut on your home page. Just tap the shortcut
to increment the counter.

That's all it is. It's my first app. It probably has bugs.
